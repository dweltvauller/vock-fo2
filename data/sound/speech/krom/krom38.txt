Ha ha ha. Yes, I suppose I've been through a lot. I come from a tribe far to the north. When I was fifteen, we were attacked by savages. I awoke that day as a boy, but by the time the sun set on that grim scene, I had slayed my way to manhood. Every man, woman, and child was dead, except for me. Ah, what's life without battle and strife? It either breaks a man, or it forges him into steel. I wear my scars with pride.
