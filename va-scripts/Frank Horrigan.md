# Frank Horrigan — VA Script
**Character:** Frank Horrigan, the Enclave's mutated super-soldier and Secret Service agent, the final boss

**Total recordable lines:** 16 (`ssa8`–`ssa23`)

> **Casting note:** Horrigan's boss-fight dialogue (`ssa2`–`ssa7`) was recorded for the original Fallout 2. These 16 lines are floats the original game never voiced. Ideally they're recorded by the original actor so Horrigan has one voice throughout; if that isn't possible, match the original recordings as closely as you can.

---

> **Direction:** A towering, heavily mutated Enclave enforcer with a deep, booming voice. Utterly contemptuous of anyone he considers a mutant, which is everyone outside the Enclave. Cruel, smug and casual about killing; he enjoys it. Even his insults are delivered with flat, bored menace rather than shouting, except when he laughs.

---

## Boss Fight Barks (Enclave)

*Shouted at the player during the final fight, one at a time, picked at random.*

`ssa8:` You're not getting out of here alive, mutie.

`ssa9:` You damn mutant.

`ssa10:` Red mutant!

`ssa11:` You've destroyed us all.

`ssa12:` You're going to pay for what you've done.

## Killing Gruthar (Vault 13)

*A cutscene. Horrigan has come to Vault 13 to wipe out the deathclaws, and Gruthar is the last one left. Cold, smug, then he kills him. Gruthar's lines are shown as cues.*

`ssa13:` You are the last, Gruthar. The stain of your kind will soon be wiped clean.

*Gruthar: "You tell your so-called President that he will burn for this."*

`ssa14:` Whatever. You were never anything more than a talking animal.

*Gruthar: "That may be, but we were created through your experimentation. You gave us life!"*

`ssa15:` And now I give you death.

*Gruthar is killed. Horrigan, to his men:*

`ssa16:` Take his body. The doctor wanted a specimen.

## Executing Matthew (San Francisco)

*A cutscene (the ACE computer vignette). Two days after the player finishes the Brotherhood of Steel quest, Horrigan and two Enclave guards corner Matthew, the Brotherhood's man in San Francisco, who believes the Chosen One will stop them. Horrigan toys with him, lets him grab a plasma rifle, laughs when the shots don't even hurt him, then punches a hole through him. Matthew's and the guards' lines are shown as cues.*

*Matthew: "You and your so-called President will not succeed."*

`ssa17:` Is that so?

*Matthew: "The Chosen One has come, and he will rid the world of your evil."*

`ssa18:` I care nothing for your pathetic prophecies. Worse, you have become a nuisance through your meddling. That ends today!

*Matthew: "Killing me will stop nothing."*

`ssa19:` That may be, but seeing your body rotting on the ground will bring a smile to my face.

*Matthew grabs a plasma rifle.*

`ssa20:` What do you think you're doing now?

*Matthew shoots him twice. It barely registers.*

`ssa21:` HA HA HA! You pathetic fool! HA HA HA!

`ssa22:` Wow, I haven't laughed like that for a while. Anyway, get ready to die.

*Matthew: "...peep..."*

*Horrigan walks up and punches a hole through him. Then he turns to the guards, casual, as if nothing happened:*

`ssa23:` Well, it's a little past 12. Anyone up for lunch?

*Guard: "Score, I'm there."*

*Guard: "Yeah, count me in on that."*
