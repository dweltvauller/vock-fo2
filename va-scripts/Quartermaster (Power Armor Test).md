# Quartermaster (Power Armor Test) — VA Script
**Character:** A second Quartermaster (QM2), Navarro military base — runs his own identity check on anyone claiming to be a new recruit

**Total recordable lines:** 18 (`qm21`–`qm218`)

---

> **Direction:** Paranoid, short-tempered, and quick to assume the worst about anyone in front of him. Muttering, put-upon asides (written in parentheses) should land as half-audible grumbling rather than clear speech. He runs his own improvised "prove you belong here" quiz and has zero patience for cute or evasive answers — sarcastic and insulting when annoyed ("Clown, are you?"), flat-out threatening once he decides you're an infiltrator. The radio-static lines are him calling for backup over a busted or fading radio — play those as garbled, urgent shouting cut off mid-word.

---

## First Challenge
*Approaching him for the first time.*

`qm21:` Who are you? What are you doing here?

*A vague or confused answer gets the same challenge repeated.*

`qm212:` You deaf? Who are you?

## Asked to Explain Yourself
*He decides to actually hear you out.*

`qm213:` New guy, eh? Suppose you want your issue. Why don't anybody ever tell me these things? What's your name?

## The Sign-Here Brush-Off
*A muttered aside — he's already decided you're just here to sign for a suit and waves you off without really looking up.*

`qm22:` (What, are you deaf? I said I got a suit; just sign here.)

*Coming back to this same brush-off a second time — his patience is gone.*

`qm23:` Clown, are you? Well, I don't need this. You explain to Sarge why you don't have a uniform!

## The Name-and-Unit Quiz
`qm24:` Okay, what's your name?

`qm25:` Right. Unit?

*Suspicious of an evasive or joke answer about the unit number.*

`qm26:` That with an S or a C?

*Stalling instead of answering.*

`qm27:` You'll get the damn armor when I'm good and ready! Now, answer the question.

*Losing patience with continued stalling.*

`qm28:` Look, clown, don't be making trouble or I'll have your insubordinate ass up on report. Just answer the question.

## Answering "Unit?" a Second Way

`qm211:` Right, J. Okay, Unit?

## Quiz Failed — Kicked Out
*Fails to give a real unit number.*

`qm29:` That's it! Get your ass over to your sergeant. I'm sending you up on charges. Dismissed.

## Quiz Passed — Gear Issued
*Gives the correct unit number.*

`qm210:` The 42nd, eh? Good unit. Okay. Here's your gear. Dismissed, soldier.

## Confused Response
*Triggered by a low-intelligence PC — he doesn't even bother with the usual quiz.*

`qm214:` What do you want?

## Blown Cover — Calling the Guards
*Ambient float, no dialog box. He's just realized he's talking to a tribal or an obvious outsider.*

`qm217:` Arroy-- Wait a minute! We just hit that place. You're a damn tribal! Guards!

`qm218:` Wait a minute -- you're not one of us! GUARDS!

## Radio Static — Calling for Backup
*Ambient float, no dialog box. Plays on a later encounter, once he's already called this in once before — a garbled follow-up over the radio.*

`qm215:` <Skzzzt-spork> -the armory!

*An earlier stage of the same radio call, cutting off mid-word.*

`qm216:` What the- <zzzzaaark!>>
