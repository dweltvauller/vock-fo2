# Marge LeBarge — VA Script
**Character:** Marge LeBarge, owner of the Kokoweef Mine, Redding

**Total recordable lines:** 40 (`marge1`–`marge40`)

---

> **Direction:** Marge is a burly, middle-aged mine owner — tough, durable, and used to being in charge of a crew of miners. She's blunt and businesslike, quick with a dismissive jab at anyone who wastes her time, but she warms up fast and genuinely once you've actually helped her out. She calls people "sweetheart" and "pal," haggles without apology, and has zero patience for the New Reno-backed Morningstar Mine crowd, though she's fiercely loyal to her own miners even when they've screwed up. Vocal direction: gravelly, no-nonsense, working-class authority — a boss who's spent her life underground and outdoors, not a diplomat. Sarcasm plays dry, not cruel; warmth, when it shows, is earned and a little reluctant.

---
