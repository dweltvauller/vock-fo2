# Typhon — VA Script
**Character:** Typhon, an old ghoul in Broken Hills who tends Seymour, a talking plant

**Total recordable lines:** 22 (`typhn29`–`typhn50`)

> **Casting note:** Typhon's dialogue (`TYPHN1`–`TYPHN28`) was recorded for the Talking Heads (THAT) mod. These 22 lines are floats THAT never voiced. Ideally they're recorded by the same actor so Typhon has one voice throughout; if that isn't possible, match the existing THAT recordings as closely as you can.

---

> **Direction:** A cranky, salty old ghoul with a gravelly voice and a dirty sense of humor. He and Seymour bicker like an old married couple: Typhon needles the plant, groans at his puns, and tells bad jokes of his own. These are floats spoken over the action, so keep them quick and conversational.
