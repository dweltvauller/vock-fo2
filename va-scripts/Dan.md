# Dan — VA Script
**Character:** Dan, worried husband of Broken Hills

**Total recordable lines:** 18 (`danbh1`–`danbh18`)

---

> **Direction:** Adult male, well-dressed but visibly rattled. He opens defensive and suspicious (a stranger's in his house), but the moment his missing wife comes up he turns pleading and needy, on the edge of tears. Once he learns she's dead, he collapses into open grief, then curdles into paranoid fear once he learns why she was killed. If the player walks away from him, he turns bitter and hateful instead. Play the arc as defensive → pleading → grief-stricken → paranoid.

---

## Confronted in His House

`danbh1:` Who are you? What are you doing in my house?

## Brush-Off for a Slow-Witted Visitor
*Response when the player picks the dim-witted greeting option.*

`danbh2:` Not in my house, please. There's a street outside where you can sleep and do whatever else it is you "special" people do.

## Getting to the Point

`danbh3:` Great, great. Pleased to meet you. [He looks distracted.] Hey, look... you're an adventuring type, right?

`danbh4:` The reason I ask, you see, is because my wife is missing. I don't know where she is. She's been gone for days. I've asked around town, and no one has seen her. Can you find her for me?

## Sweetening the Offer
*If the player asks what's in it for them.*

`danbh5:` I can give you $500. That's my life savings.

## Begging
*If the player still refuses to help.*

`danbh6:` Please. Please. My wife... she's everything to me. Please.

## Handing Over the Money

`danbh7:` Here's the money. Now please, please, PLEASE... find my wife and tell me where she is. Maybe she's shacked up with someone else, maybe she's left me... but I just need to know.

## Turned Down Flat
*If the player refuses to help even after he begs. He doesn't take it well.*

`danbh8:` ::he sobs uncontrollably for a few moments:: Very well. Go away. And realize that you've broken my heart.

## Describing Her

`danbh9:` She's blonde, sexy, and has the most incredible singing voice... I last saw her walking around this area. She didn't like mutants or ghouls, so she wouldn't have been near any of them. Nobody knows where she went. It's like she just disappeared.

## Checking In
*Asked on later visits while the search is still ongoing.*

`danbh10:` Well? Have you found her? My heart aches with every moment.

## Still Waiting
*If the player says they're still looking, or gives up on the search.*

`danbh11:` Oh, my aching heart. Please let me know when you find her.

## She's Been Found

`danbh12:` You found her? ::his eyes light up:: Where?

## Refusing to Believe the Worst
*Reacts with denial to a crude, dismissive way of breaking the news.*

`danbh13:` What? No! She had great legs!

## Grief

`danbh14:` Thank you for telling me. I think I have to go cry.

## The Truth About Her

`danbh15:` Oh my God! I... I can't believe that... It's not... [Sighs deeply]. I suppose it may be true. She seemed to hate every mutant and ghoul she came into contact with. I never knew why.

## Fearing for His Own Life

`danbh16:` You don't mean to say that my life is in danger too? Oh dear. You may be right. I've been hearing noises outside the house at night. Whispers and shuffles.

## Hostile Brush-Off
*Once he's given up on the player, or been rejected outright.*

`danbh17:` I hate you! Everyone hates you! Go die! ::sob::

## Leaving Town
*Said once he's decided to flee Broken Hills for good.*

`danbh18:` Thanks so much for your help. I'm not staying here any longer than I have to.
