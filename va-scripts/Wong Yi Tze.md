# Wong Yi Tze — VA Script
**Character:** Dr. Wong Yi Tze, head scientist of the Shi, San Francisco

**Total recordable lines:** 53 (`wong1`–`wong53`)

---

> **Direction:** An older, short and rotund Shi scientist and bureaucrat: dry, precise, and openly proud of his position. Calm and faintly amused under pressure rather than rattled — he deflects threats with polite menace ("that might be detrimental to your health") and drops a dark little joke about eating a stolen spleen with fava beans and a nice Chianti without missing a beat. Protective of Shi secrets and willing to call guards the moment he decides the conversation isn't worth his time. Businesslike and a little weary once the Dr. Sheng storyline comes up — he clearly regrets ever trusting his predecessor, is glad to be rid of the problem, and doesn't pretend otherwise. Two short reactive floats break character entirely: genuine pain and fear if he's poisoned.

---
