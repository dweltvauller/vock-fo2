# Enclave Squad Leader — VA Script
**Character:** the leader of an Enclave squad, met in a desert random encounter

**Total recordable lines:** 4 (`encld1`–`encld4`)

---

> **Direction:** A huge, cold soldier in power armor. He doesn't raise his voice and doesn't need to. Each line is flatter and more final than the last. "Kill them all" is an order, not a threat, given without emotion. He speaks to the player the same way, as a dismissal.

## The Old Man in the Desert

`encld1:` Old man, you WILL come with us.

*Old man: "Leave us alone. I will never support the cause of your superiors."*

`encld2:` I will not ask you again. Come now, or you will be made an example of.

*Old man: "I would rather what knowledge I have disappear with me into the grave."*

`encld3:` So be it. Kill them all.

*His men open fire. Then he turns to the player:*

`encld4:` This is none of your affair. Turn around and walk away.
