# Ethyl Wright — VA Script
**Character:** Mrs. Ethyl Wright, head of the Temperance Union, New Reno (ncethwri)

**Total recordable lines:** 67 (`ew1`–`ew67`)

---

> **Direction:** A plump, proper, matronly busybody in her middle years — head of New Reno's Temperance Union and the self-appointed moral conscience of a city built on sin. She's easily flustered and jumps when startled, prone to dramatic, wagging-finger sermons about the evils of "firewater," and condescending toward "tribals" in a way she thinks is gracious hospitality. Underneath the primness she's genuinely warm-hearted (quick to press her own belongings into a stranger's hands out of pity). Her arc: proper and moralizing at first contact, then devastated and betrayed when she learns her own husband's still was never dismantled, then hardening into steely resolve, and finally warm gratitude once the still is destroyed.

---
