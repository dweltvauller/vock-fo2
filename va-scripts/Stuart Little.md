# Stuart Little — VA Script
**Character:** Stuart Little, the fast-talking midget boxing agent at the New Reno Jungle Gym

**Total recordable lines:** 17 (`stul66`–`stul82`)

> **Casting note:** Stuart Little's dialogue (`STUL1`–`STUL65`) was recorded for the Talking Heads (THAT) mod. These lines are floats THAT never voiced. Ideally they're recorded by the same actor so the character has one voice throughout; if that isn't possible, match the existing THAT recordings as closely as you can.

---

> **Direction:** A pint-sized, motor-mouthed fight promoter: brash, salty, a bit of a hustler, but genuinely proud of his fighters. Quick, punchy delivery.
