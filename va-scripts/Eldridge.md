# Eldridge — VA Script
**Character:** Eldridge, Owner of New Reno Arms (New Reno)

**Total recordable lines:** 93 (`eld1`–`eld93`)

---

> **Direction:** Adult male, backwoods/redneck gun nut — thick down-home dialect ("awright," "y'hear," "ain't," "innit"), gleeful and a little unhinged about weapons ("gazing at his weapon collection and smiling dreamily"). Plays as a friendly, fast-talking shopkeeper with customers — glad-handing, always closing a sale, willing to haggle. That same folksy warmth turns sharp and threatening fast when someone messes with his door, wanders into the back room, or shows up after closing — think shotgun-behind-the-counter energy. No long arc; it's situational: warm salesman with a paying customer, hard-edged guard dog the moment his property's at risk.
