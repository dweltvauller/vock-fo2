# Jo — VA Script
**Character:** Jo, trading post owner (and de facto sheriff, mayor, and judge), Modoc

**Total recordable lines:** 86 (`jomod1`–`jomod86`)

---

> **Direction:** Middle-aged frontier everyman, folksy and a little worn down — he's trying to run a trading post, keep the peace, and hold Modoc together through a bad drought, all at once, and it shows. Warm and welcoming at baseline, with a dry, self-deprecating sense of humor about how much he's stretched thin ("I run this here trading post... and I'm the sheriff, mayor, judge, and any other official you can think of"). Capable of real gallows humor and a startling flash of menace (the pinky-finger bluff). Turns hard, cold, or openly angry when Modoc's survival is on the line or when he feels the PC has handled things badly. Also moonlights as the town's deadpan wedding officiant.

---
