# Stark — VA Script
**Character:** Sergeant Stark, head of the Vault City guard in the Corrections Center

**Total recordable lines:** 15 (`stark111`–`stark125`)

> **Casting note:** Stark's dialogue (`STARK1`–`STARK110`) was recorded for the Talking Heads (THAT) mod. These lines are floats THAT never voiced. Ideally they're recorded by the same actor so the character has one voice throughout; if that isn't possible, match the existing THAT recordings as closely as you can.

---

> **Direction:** A by-the-book Vault City guard sergeant: stiff, curt and proud of the city, polite to citizens, contemptuous of outsiders, mutants and ghouls.

---
