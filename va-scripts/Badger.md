# Badger — VA Script
**Character:** Badger, hacker punk, San Francisco (the tanker crew)

**Total recordable lines:** 42 (`badge1`–`badge42`)

---

> **Direction:** Adult male punk, foul-mouthed and gross-out funny — belching contests, farting, cursing at people who annoy him. Underneath the gross-out act he's the crew's actual computer genius, and once he warms up to the PC he's a genuinely useful, almost eager ally, happy to hack rival factions for a favor. He has a real soft spot for his girlfriend that surfaces as panic and grief if she's in danger, and relief/gratitude if she's saved. If the PC turns on the Shi and gets him burned as a result, he flips to bitter and threatening. Play the arc as gross-out slacker → warm and helpful → (if betrayed) scared, then vengeful.
