# Fannie Mae — VA Script
**Character:** Fannie Mae, hostess at the Malamute Saloon, Redding

**Total recordable lines:** 39 (`fanni1`–`fanni39`)

---

> **Direction:** Fannie Mae is a saloon girl in Redding, worn down and desperate, deep in a spiral of Jet addiction. She's world-weary and rough around the edges — flirtatious and playful with customers on a good day, but underneath it she's scared, sick, and slowly killing herself. Vocal qualities: raspy, world-worn Old West drawl, quick with a flirty come-on but with real exhaustion and sickness bleeding through when she's craving Jet. She dreams of paying off her debt to "Lou" (her madam) so she and her boyfriend can leave this life — that hope should come through as genuine, almost desperate, whenever it comes up. Once she's finally been cured and thanks the PC, let real warmth and relief show through for the first time.

---
