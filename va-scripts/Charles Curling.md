# Charles Curling — VA Script
**Character:** Lt. Col. Dr. Charles Curling, head of the Enclave's Chemical Corps, Enclave Oil Rig

**Total recordable lines:** 77 (`curl1`–`curl77`)

---

> **Direction:** Older adult male, career military-scientist. Clipped, precise diction — he introduces himself by full rank and title and expects the same formality back. Coldly intellectual and condescending toward "mutants," genuinely convinced his genocidal research is rational, even noble, necessity — play the ideology as sincere, not cartoonish villainy. He stays detached and superior through most of the conversation, but a determined, persuasive PC can crack that: once he's actually convinced he's wrong, the anguish should sound real and immediate, not like a switch flipping — a proud man forced to confront that his life's work was monstrous.
