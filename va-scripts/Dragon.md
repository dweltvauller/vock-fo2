# Dragon — VA Script
**Character:** The Dragon, martial-arts master and teacher in San Francisco's Chinatown, sworn enemy of Lo Pan

**Total recordable lines:** 11 (`drago35`–`drago45`)

> **Casting note:** The Dragon's dialogue (`DRAGO1`–`DRAGO34`) was recorded for the Talking Heads (THAT) mod. These 11 lines are the floats THAT never voiced. Ideally they're recorded by the same actor so the Dragon has one voice throughout; if that isn't possible, match the existing THAT recordings as closely as you can.

---

> **Direction:** Adult male, a calm and disciplined martial-arts master. Measured, formal, a little old-fashioned in his phrasing, never loud for the sake of it. Honorable and sure of himself, with a teacher's patience toward students and quiet contempt for anyone without principle. These are short floats spoken over the action, so keep them clean and compact.

---
