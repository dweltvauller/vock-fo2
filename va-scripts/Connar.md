# Connar — VA Script
**Character:** Connar, Leader of the Vault Village (mole-rat hunters outside Vault City)

**Total recordable lines:** 52 (`cnr1`–`cnr52`)

---

> **Direction:** Adult male, rough and wary -- a squatter-camp leader who's used to being threatened by Vault City and expects everyone else to be a threat too. Guarded and suspicious on first contact, quick to snap, but underneath it he's protective of his people and genuinely grateful to anyone who actually helps them. Turns cold and vengeful if he feels betrayed or swindled, and if his village is ever crushed and he ends up hauled off to Vault City himself, that toughness cracks into real fear and pleading.
