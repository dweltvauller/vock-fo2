# John Bishop — VA Script
**Character:** John Bishop, patriarch of the Bishop crime family, New Reno

**Total recordable lines:** 109 (`bishp1`–`bishp109`)

---

> **Direction:** Bishop is a powerful, grim-faced middle-aged crime boss who built his family's empire on murder-for-hire and does not pretend otherwise. He is blunt, foul-mouthed, and businesslike about killing — he talks about a hit the way another man talks about a delivery schedule. He calls the PC "tribal" constantly, sometimes with grudging respect, more often with contempt. Underneath the vulgarity he's a careful negotiator: he never raises the price without a fight, and he never forgets a slight. He turns cold and genuinely dangerous the moment anyone touches his wife or daughter — the mask of businesslike calm drops completely and what's underneath is possessive rage, not performance. No accent direction has been recorded for him; play him as a hard, weathered American crime boss.
