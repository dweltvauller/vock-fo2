# Anna Winslow — VA Script
**Character:** Anna Winslow, a child ghost haunting a house in the Den

**Total recordable lines:** 15 (`anna1`–`anna15`)

---

> **Direction:** Anna is a young girl's ghost, stuck in a loop of pain and confusion, searching for a locket she's lost and calling for her parents. She doesn't fully register that she's dead or that decades have passed — to her, this only just happened. Play her as disoriented and wounded, flickering between crying pain, plaintive searching, and sudden sharp accusation ("Thief! Thief!") when she thinks someone's taken her locket. If the player returns it, she softens completely — grateful, and finally ready to let go, drifting from relief into a hushed, otherworldly calm as she passes into the light and reunites with her family.
