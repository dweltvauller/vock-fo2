# Shaman — VA Script
**Character:** the Primitive Tribe's young shaman (Umbra Tribe)

**Total recordable lines:** 4 (`sham21`–`sham24`)

> **Casting note:** The Shaman's dialogue (`SHAM1`–`SHAM20`) was recorded for the Talking Heads (THAT) mod. These 4 lines are a cutscene THAT never voiced. Ideally they're recorded by the same actor so the Shaman has one voice throughout; if that isn't possible, match the existing THAT recordings as closely as you can.

---

> **Direction:** A young man who was only ever an apprentice, now the tribe's shaman by default. He talks big but doesn't trust himself. In this scene he starts out panicking under his breath, then finds his nerve and speaks to the ghost with real, gentle authority.

---

## Laying Nessa to Rest (Primitive Tribe)

*A cutscene. The player has learned who killed Nessa, the girl whose ghost haunts the village, and brings the Shaman to the fire to guide her spirit on. Nessa's lines are shown as cues.*

*Under his breath, nerves first, then steeling himself:*

`sham21:` Oh, shit... oh, shit... No, I can do this... Okay. Nessa?

*Nessa: "Ohhh... So cold and alone..."*

`sham22:` Nessa, you must hear my voice. I am calling to you from the mortal world.

*Nessa: "I... I hear someone! Who is there?"*

`sham23:` I am the shaman of this village. You must enter the mists. There, you will find peace.

*Nessa: "Yes! Yes I see the mists now. I see spirits beckoning to me."*

`sham24:` Go to them, Nessa. Go into the mists and join your ancestors.

*Nessa: "I'm not alone anymore."*

---

*End of script — 4 lines. Tag numbers continue after THAT's `SHAM20`.*
