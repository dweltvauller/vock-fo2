# Dr. Fung — VA Script
**Character:** Dr. Fung, Shi doctor and teacher, San Francisco

**Total recordable lines:** 36 (`fung1`–`fung36`)

---

> **Direction:** A wizened old Shi doctor in a white smock — courtly, businesslike, and completely unsentimental about money. He heals anyone who can pay, quotes his prices with the same calm whether the wound is a scratch or a shattered limb, and turns cheerfully mercenary the moment implants or surgery come up ("Sit down. You'll need to when you hear my bill."). Warm and a little sly about his old student Doc Holliday, and openly proud of Shi history and science. Two short, cold lines break the surgeon's-bedside-manner tone entirely: flat refusals to treat anyone who's crossed his people.

---

`fung1:` Help you with something?

## Looking Over an Injury

`fung2:` Let me take a look at that. Hmm. Yes. I'll have to charge you, of course.

`fung5:` He went home, did he. I hope he has found peace there.

`fung11:` What? You do not have the money? How do you expect me to survive if you ask me for free healing?

## After Bartering
*One or the other, depending on how the trade goes.*

`fung12:` It has been a pleasure doing business with you.

## Return Visit

`fung16:` You need more?

## Asked About San Francisco

`fung17:` You have come to the right person. Would you like long history or short?

## Short Version

`fung18:` Our people landed here on the submarine Shih-huang-ti, when the missiles exploded over the world. With the aid of a cult, we, the Shi, rebuilt San Francisco. That is the short version.

## Long Version
*Three-part telling.*

`fung19:` The long version is this: We are here because our people are the descendants of the crew of a nuclear submarine, called the Shih-huang-ti. When the missiles fell in the Great Deluge, the systems aboard the submarine failed and we drifted in the dark for many days.

`fung20:` At last, we drifted ashore. We found ourselves near the ruins of San Francisco. Most of the city was devastated in the war. We had to recreate society based on the few survivors who had crept back to the city.

`fung21:` We rebuilt with what we could find, and we have scientists and technology the world must envy. We are rebuilding civilization, but not as that horrid Master creature did.

## Agreeing to the Spleen Surgery
*Plays when he already knows the PC as a friend of Doc Holliday — does it as a favor.*

`fung22:` Of course I can. And since you are aware of Doc Holliday... I will do it for no more than the sake of friendship. Tell your spleenless friend to come here and I will replace it for him.

## Agreeing to the Spleen Surgery, For a Price

`fung23:` Yes. I can do this. It will cost you $1000 dollars to do it. Up front. Do you accept?

`fung24:` Tell your friend to come here and I will replace it for him.

## Refusing Until Paid

`fung25:` I can do nothing for him, until you have the $1000. Good day.

## Asked About Armor Implants
*Two takes — the first time, and any time after.*

`fung26:` I helped write the article on armor implants. All it takes is my expertise and your materials. What types of implants do you desire?

`fung34:` Are you interested again in implants?

## Explaining the Implants

`fung27:` We have dermal implants, which protect against concussive damage. If you like that work, we can add a single enhancement to it. We also have Phoenix implants, which protect against heat-based attacks, and which also have an upgrade available. If you're interested in the upgrades, you will of course have to wait until the initial set has been installed. And before you ask, yes, you CAN have all four operations.

## Pricing the Dermal Implants

`fung28:` Excellent. I'll need a set of combat armor and, oh, $5500. You'll need two days and the ability not to pick at scabs.

## Pricing the Dermal Upgrade

`fung29:` The dermal upgrade, eh? That will cost you several days of your life, $33000, combat armor, and a brand-new set of scars that you'll never ever lose as long as you live.

## Pricing the Phoenix Implants

`fung30:` If you do not mind parting with $8500, a few days, and a set of combat armor, we can begin the operation now.

## Pricing the Phoenix Upgrade

`fung31:` This is a major operation. It will cost you your "good looks", a set of combat armor, and $49000. Are you willing to invest in this?

## Turned Away, Missing Requirements

`fung32:` ::he shakes his head:: Do not toy with me. You are missing necessary ingredients. Listen more carefully to me next time. Now go, and come back when I can help you.

## Operation Complete

`fung33:` There you go! You are ... enhanced. Remember... do not pick at the scabs unless you want horrid scarring to add to your suddenly not-entirely-pleasing appearance.

## Refusing an Enemy of the Shi

`fung35:` I do not treat the enemies of my people. My humble apologies.

## Refusing an Elron Ally
*Same coldness, aimed at anyone the Shi consider an enemy through the Elron/Hubologist conflict.*

`fung36:` Honored sir, I recommend you seek out your cult's doctors.
