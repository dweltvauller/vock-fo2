# Francis — VA Script
**Character:** Francis, Mutant Arm Wrestler, Broken Hills (hcfranci)

**Total recordable lines:** 43 (`franc1`–`franc43`)

---

> **Direction:** Adult-sounding super mutant — big, physical, prideful of his strength, and a little bored most of the time. Blue-collar bruiser with a code: blunt, plain-spoken ("pal," "buddy"), quick to challenge anyone over an arm-wrestle, and genuinely warm to people who earn his respect (buying a hero a drink, handing over his prize fist). Turns cold and threatening fast if the player's reputation is bad or he's cornered — no hesitation, no theatrics, just a flat promise of violence. Late in his arc he's revealed as a vigilante who quietly killed people threatening Broken Hills' human/mutant peace; play that reveal unrepentant and matter-of-fact, not guilty — he believes he did the right thing, explains himself once, and leaves town without complaint.

---

`franc29:` Innocents? Ha! The people I killed were trying to kill ME! I just returned the favor and made sure their deaths served another purpose - one that might further the cause of peace.

`franc30:` If you talked to some of the people in town about your hatred of mutants, you'd find out about the conspiracy. I know two wrongs don't make a right, but I call it self-defense. I did something wrong, and I admit it. I can't stay here. I'll set out to find a way to atone for my actions... That satisfy your sense of justice?
