# Raul — VA Script
**Character:** Raul, Vertibird Mechanic at Navarro

**Total recordable lines:** 27 (`raul1`–`raul27`)

---

> **Direction:** Adult male, a Navarro base mechanic who speaks in blunt, broken English -- dropped articles, third-person self-reference ("Raul don't want to see him"). Gruff but not unfriendly by default, with a short fuse and a possessive streak over "his birds" (the vertibirds he keeps running). Escalates fast from irritated brush-off to genuine fury the moment someone messes with his planes, and switches into alert, on-edge soldier mode the moment the base is under attack.
