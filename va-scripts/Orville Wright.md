# Orville Wright — VA Script
**Character:** Orville Wright, patriarch of the Wright crime family, New Reno

**Total recordable lines:** 81 (`write1`–`write80`)

---

> **Direction:** Orville Wright is an older, gray-haired crime boss running a bootleg-liquor operation out of New Reno. He is perpetually irritated — quick to scowl, quick to shout for his boys, and openly grieving a son (Richard) he believes was murdered. His default tone toward strangers is suspicious and gruff, addressing outsiders as "tribal" (or "slaver," if he's pegged them as one) until they've proven themselves. Once a job is done well he warms slightly, though he's still rough around the edges. He swears often and isn't shy about threatening violence. Deep, weathered voice with plenty of gravel in it — this is a man used to being obeyed.
