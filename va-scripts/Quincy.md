# Quincy — VA Script
**Character:** Quincy, maintenance mechanic at the Navarro military base

**Total recordable lines:** 29 (`quin1`–`quin29`)

---

> **Direction:** Quincy is a Navarro grunt turned mechanic — gruff, no-nonsense, more interested in his inventory than in visitors. He's suspicious of strangers on base by default, quick to go from bored irritation to genuine alarm if he thinks he's talking to a spy. Vocal qualities: clipped military cadence, a working-class edge, sarcastic ("Einstein") when he thinks he's dealing with an idiot. He can flip fast — dismissive and put-upon one moment, shouting for guards the next — and slips into a harder edge when threatened at gunpoint.

---

## Main Hub

*Coming back to this hub later in the same conversation.*

`quin3:` What do you need to know?
