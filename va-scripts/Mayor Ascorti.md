# Mayor Ascorti — VA Script
**Character:** Ascorti, Mayor of Redding and owner of the town's casino

**Total recordable lines:** 60 (`ascor1`–`ascor60`)

---

> **Direction:** Middle-aged male, a smooth-talking businessman with a folksy frontier turn of phrase ("heck," "cain't," "if'n"). Congenial and a little oily whenever there's money or a deal on the table, and quick to frame his own self-interest as civic duty. That charm has a hard edge underneath it -- the moment he feels disrespected, cheated, or threatened, he turns cold, then openly hostile, up to calling for his guards.
