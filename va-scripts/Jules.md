# Jules -- VA Script
**Character:** Jules, freelance New Reno tour guide, pimp, and Jet dealer working the town's entrance

**Total recordable lines:** 198 (`jules1`-`jules198`)

---

> **Direction:** Adult male, a fast-talking New Reno hustler who works the town's front door -- part tour guide, part pimp, part drug dealer, always angling for a cut. He's a professional charmer: smooth, hyperbolic, quick with "brother"/"sister"/"friend," and never short on a sales pitch, whether he's selling Jet, women, or just goodwill. Underneath the patter he's genuinely streetwise and a little dangerous -- he's mixed up in a stolen-car/hit-and-run plot with a kid named Cody, and when the PC corners him about it his easy charm curdles fast: first he plays dumb, then he gets defensive and threatening, then -- once he's caught -- he folds and turns nervous and placating. Play the arc as huckster -> cornered liar -> scared and compliant.
