# Doctor Jubilee

NCR 1, the hospital.

**54 recordable lines, tags jub1-jub54.**

> An older man, gruff and folksy but decent underneath it, worn down by running the town's only real hospital on no budget and no help. Patient with the sick, short with time-wasters, and fiercely protective of the medicine on his shelf. His voice should carry a tired, been-doing-this-forever warmth, quick to needle a patient ("sonny," "young man") but quicker to turn stern the moment someone reaches for his supplies.
