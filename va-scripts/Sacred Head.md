# Sacred Head — VA Script
**Character:** The Sacred Head of the Vault Dweller — a giant talking stone head half buried in the desert, special random encounter

**Total recordable lines:** 44 (`bhead1`–`bhead44`)

---

> **Direction:** A giant stone head of the Vault Dweller, stuck in the desert sand, bored out of its mind. It has two voices. Its real self is a grumpy, petty, sarcastic old grump who mutters to himself, snores, and talks in his sleep like a kid who won't get up for school. When someone disturbs it, it puts on the voice of a god: deep, booming, slow and echoing, every line in capitals delivered like a thunderclap. That act keeps cracking. It dissolves into a childish "Uh-uh" argument, runs out of steam after twelve hours of it, and drops straight back into the bored grump the moment the PC gets the gift. Think the Great and Powerful Oz, before and after the curtain is pulled back, or the Easter Island head from Night at the Museum. Comic timing matters most in the argument. Feel free to give it your own spin!

---

## Muttering to Himself

`bhead10:` If only someone would come along and dig the rest of me up...

`bhead11:` I'm sure those stone-eating ants will be along at any moment to devour me.

`bhead12:` "Be a big stone head! See the world!" What hogwash.

`bhead13:` It is a mystery to me how I ended up out here in the desert.

`bhead14:` I still don't see why I have to sit out here in the desert, while that other head gets to hang out at the village.

`bhead15:` I mean, he gets worshipped and cleaned and prayed to... all I get is to look at sand all day.

`bhead16:` I hear those guys at Easter Island have it pretty good.

`bhead17:` I bet Stonehenge gets more visitors than me.

## Woken Up
*The PC knocks on the head. At night it is asleep and mumbles through `bhead18`–`bhead20` as the PC keeps knocking; by day it starts at `bhead21`. Half asleep, then startled awake, then outraged. The PC steps back.*

`bhead18:` Hrnnfff... uh-uh, Mom... therez no school today... Zsnzzzzz

`bhead19:` Zzzsnnnzzzz... I don wanna get up yet....Zzznnsss

`bhead20:` Zzsssccs... eh?

`bhead21:` Wha--?

`bhead22:` GET YOUR DAMN HANDS OFFA ME!

## The Challenge
*It composes itself, clears its throat, and puts on the god voice for the first time.*

`bhead23:` *Ahem*

`bhead24:` WHO DARES APPROACH THE SACRED HEAD OF THE VAULT DWELLER?!

## "You? The Chosen One?"
*The PC: "It is I, the Chosen One." The god voice laughs it off: a huge, rolling laugh, then mocking.*

`bhead25:` HA HA HA HA HA HA!

`bhead26:` *YOU*?! THE CHOSEN ONE?!

`bhead27:` YOU ARE NO BIGGER THAN MY FINGER!

`bhead28:` YOU ARE *NOT* THE CHOSEN ONE.

## The Argument
*The PC keeps answering "Yes, I am." / "Uh-huh." Each reply is shorter and more stubborn; the god voice is starting to sound like a sulking child. Two separate takes for the two "UH-UH."s, the second more petulant.*

`bhead29:` NO, YOU'RE NOT.

`bhead30:` UH-UH.

`bhead31:` UH-UH.

## Twelve Hours Later
*Twelve hours of "Uh-huh" / "Uh-uh" have passed. Exhausted, hoarse, barely able to keep it going.*

`bhead32:` UH... UH...

## Giving In
*It gives up. `bhead33` is the last gasp of a fed-up grump; then it pulls the god voice back together for a grand, ceremonial verdict, and hands over the gift. `bhead37` is a little too eager to be rid of the PC.*

`bhead33:` UH... ENOUGH OF THIS!

`bhead34:` VERY WELL! YOU HAVE PROVEN YOURSELF!

`bhead35:` YOU *ARE* THE CHOSEN ONE!

`bhead36:` TAKE THIS!

`bhead37:` NOW LEAVE ME!

## Get Out
*The PC: "Hey, thanks!" The act drops instantly: flat, bored, done.*

`bhead38:` YEAH, WHATEVER. NOW GET OUTTA HERE.

`bhead39:` DAMN TOURISTS.

## After the Gift
*Floats over the head by day once the PC has the gift. `bhead1`–`bhead3` cycle in order. Muttering, grumpy, not quite under its breath.*

`bhead1:` Damn tourists...

`bhead2:` "I'm the Chosen One." What crapola.

`bhead3:` If I were you, "Chosen One," I'd get out of here.

## Coming Back for More
*The PC knocks on the head again after getting the gift. Irritated god voice for the first two lines, then its own voice, under its breath.*

`bhead40:` I ALREADY GAVE YOU A GIFT.

`bhead41:` BEGONE!

`bhead42:` Idiot.

## Caught Stealing
*Floats over the head when the PC tries to steal from it. Full fury: the god voice for real this time. The PC is disintegrated right after `bhead44`.*

`bhead43:` WHAT?! STEAL FROM ME, WILL YOU?!

`bhead44:` DIE!
