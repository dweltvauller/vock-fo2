# Thomas Moore — VA Script
**Character:** Thomas Moore, a Citizen of Vault City

**Total recordable lines:** 40 (`moore1`–`moore40`)

---

> **Direction:** A street-corner preacher persona layered over a secret agenda — Moore rails against Vault City's selfishness and slavery in warm, evangelical language, calling everyone "brother" or "friend" and talking about "the word" like a true believer. Underneath, he's a schemer working to bring the city down from within, and that shows in flashes: sharp suspicion when he's testing whether you can be trusted, real panic when he's caught and blackmailed, and cold menace when he's cornered as a slaver or exposed as a spy. Adult male, functional preacher's cadence — passionate and rhythmic on the "word" lines, tightening into something harder and more clipped whenever he's threatened.
