# Robodog — VA Script
**Character:** Robodog, Dr. Henry's cybernetic dog, NCR

**Total recordable lines:** 33 (`dogr1`–`dogr33`)

---

> **Direction:** Robodog is a half-dog, half-machine companion built by Dr. Henry — equal parts loyal pet and malfunctioning gadget. He doesn't speak words; he barks, whines, and glitches, with mechanical clicks, whirrs, and static bursts layered over dog sounds. Play it like a sound-effects performance more than line-reading: enthusiastic and doglike at the core, with the occasional broken-electronics stutter or garbled almost-word breaking through (a mangled "Scooby-Doo" reference, a snippet of cat-sound static, a deadpan "I am not a Daggit"). Comic, endearing, never menacing — even the growls are more grumbly than threatening.
