# Professor — VA Script
**Character:** the Professor, a mad scientist in Broken Hills who tests people against his smart radscorpion

**Total recordable lines:** 20 (`prof1`–`prof20`)

---

> **Direction:** An older man in a lab coat with an insane gleam in his eye. Impatient, distracted, always busy with something else. He brightens the moment someone wants to be tested, and he's proudest of his scorpion. Quick and excitable, a little pompous, never quite listening.

---

## Greetings

*First time the player talks to him:*

`prof1:` What is it you want? Out with it! I'm a busy man!

*Every time after that:*

`prof12:` You again? What is it you want? Out with it! I'm a busy man!

## Who He Is

`prof9:` I am the Professor. What I do is applied intelligence testing. I create chemical compounds to increase intelligence. I'm trying some out on this radscorpion here, after my failure with the plant.

*Defensive, as if it's obvious:*

`prof10:` Of course I tried it out on a plant! I'm trying to increase intelligence from the ground up!

*Proud:*

`prof11:` Yes, I have, thank you very much! You ever hear of "Mentats"? I helped develop a method for easy mass-production of that drug! And others! Lots of others!

## Offering a Test

*To a player with very low intelligence (he talks down to them a little):*

`prof2:` Ah, a test subject! Would you like to take a test, my friend?

`prof3:` You wish to take a test, eh? Do you want to test your hands, your eyes, or your brains?

*To anyone else:*

`prof5:` You wish to take a test, eh? Do you want to test your manual dexterity, your perception, or your intelligence?

*If the player turns him down (gets more irritated as it goes):*

`prof4:` Then why are you wasting my time? I told you I'm busy, didn't I? I have too much to do to stand around wasting time talking to people like ... why am I still talking to you? Good day!

## The Tests

*Each test is a short cutscene: the player and the scorpion compete while the Professor referees.*

*Agility test, picking locks:*

`prof6:` Very well. I'll be matching you against the scorpion. You will both attempt to unlock those boxes. Good luck.

*If it's a tie:*

`prof14:` Well, that was a tie.

*If the player loses (the score appears on screen after this, so stop here):*

`prof20:` You lose.

*If the player won but has very low intelligence, he hands over a consolation prize:*

`prof15:` Here, take these Mentats. You appear to need them.

*Perception test, reading letters off a chart:*

`prof7:` Right. You'll stand behind this rail, and you and the scorpion will tell me the letters you read, from top to bottom, left to right.

*If the player reads them right:*

`prof16:` Yes, that's correct.

*If the player gets it wrong:*

`prof17:` Uh... no, I'm sorry. That's way off.

*Then the scorpion chitters its answer, and he's delighted with it:*

`prof18:` Correct! Good boy! Gooooood boy! Your Morse code skills are improving nicely.

*Intelligence test, chess:*

`prof8:` You and the scorpion will be playing a game of my own devising. I call it "chess". Are you ready?

*The scorpion wins and the player cries foul:*

`prof19:` Don't be a sore loser.

## His Scorpion Is Dead

*If the player has killed the scorpion. Shaken, near tears:*

`prof13:` How... how could you kill my scorpion like that? I was close... so close...

---

*End of script — 20 lines. Tag numbers follow message order, not this document's grouping.*
