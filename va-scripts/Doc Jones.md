# Doc Jones — VA Script
**Character:** Doc Jones, a doctor held prisoner in Darion's Vault 15

**Total recordable lines:** 21 (`jones1`–`jones21`)

---

> **Direction:** Doc Jones is a small, mild-mannered doctor -- gentle and professional with patients, but weary and a little bitter about his situation. He's Darion's prisoner, kept underground to tend to the old man, and he resents it even though he tries to stay civil about it. When asked about his captivity he's matter-of-fact rather than dramatic -- a man who's made peace with a bad situation but hasn't stopped wanting out. Talking about Darion himself, he turns a little sardonic ("mental case, plain and simple"), especially describing Darion's paranoia about Tandi. Once freed, he's warm and relieved, openly grateful, and glad to finally be useful to the people who actually need him. If the player is hostile to him, he's flatly unwilling to help -- no threats, just a cold refusal.
