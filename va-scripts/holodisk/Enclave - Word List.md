# Word List — VA Script

**Type:** Holodisk (Pip-Boy narration)

**Recordable clip:** 1 (`birdwrds`)

**In-universe author:** Vice-President Daniel Bird, a real interactive NPC ("a small nervous-looking man"). This is his own memo, and his live dialogue has the exact same "how do you spell potato" line, so whoever voices him should read this too.

**Wiki:** https://fallout.fandom.com/wiki/Daniel_Bird

---

> **Direction:** Completely deadpan and self-serious, he has no idea how this sounds. Same oblivious sincerity straight through, including "Jackelope," no wink.
>
> **Reading the lists:** Read each word as its own item, with a short beat between words. Most "Words to Change" sound the same as the originals ("Potatoe" is still "potato"); the joke is on screen, so do not exaggerate them. Read "Suttell" as written: "SUT-tell". "To be prepared" is in both lists on purpose, read it both times.

---
