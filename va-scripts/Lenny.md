# Lenny — VA Script
**Character:** Lenny, Harold's assistant and town doctor in Gecko (later a party member)

**Total recordable lines:** 78 (`lenny1`–`lenny78`)

---

> **Direction:** Lenny is a short, elderly ghoul who's been patching up Gecko for longer than most of its residents have been alive. He talks with a nervous, folksy stammer that never quite goes away, warm and a little self-deprecating, quick to undersell his own skills even while he's clearly good at his job. He carries real guilt about the day the original Vault Dweller passed through Necropolis — he saw a stranger running for their life and didn't have the nerve to join them, and it's haunted him ever since. That guilt turns into genuine, teary sincerity if the topic comes up, and into real emotion (relief, gratitude, wounded pride) around the choice to finally join the PC on the road. As a party member he's fussy and protective, a country-doctor bedside manner even out in the field, and openly uneasy about his own fighting ability ("I'm a healer, not a fighter"). Pushed hard enough — treated cruelly, dragged into things he finds shameful — he can turn hurt and bitter, still stammering but with real bite in it. Elderly male voice throughout; the apostrophed stutters ("Y'you," "c'can," "g'go") are written into every line and should be performed, not smoothed over.
