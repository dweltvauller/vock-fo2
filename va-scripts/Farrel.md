# Farrel — VA Script
**Character:** Farrel, elderly resident of Modoc, accused of stealing Cornelius's gold watch

**Total recordable lines:** 33 (`far1`–`far33`)

---

> **Direction:** A poor, proud old man who has clearly had this argument before and is sick of it. Wounded and self-pitying at first ("stop coming over here and harassing poor old Farrel!"), then indignant and a little theatrical as he defends his honor — he'd rather be shot than admit to something he didn't do. Warms up considerably once the PC seems willing to actually help him, becoming grateful and almost sentimental. Separately, he keeps a stock of simplified, deliberately dumbed-down "caveman" speech ("Ooga right back at ya... my slope-foreheaded little friend") that he switches into for a lower-intelligence visitor — play that as a different register entirely, broad and a little mocking, not just the same lines said slower. Underneath it all is a real anger that flares up when he's cornered or threatened — that's a different, harder edge from the wounded-pride tone of his normal dialogue.

## About Modoc

`far10:` Modoc is a small community of farmers and ranchers. We supply most of the food for the surrounding towns and cities by trading our crops for various essentials. It's actually a very nice arrangement. However, this could all change with the drought. We can barely keep ourselves fed, let alone have surplus to trade.

## The Feud, Explained

`far17:` Everybody is accusing me of stealing my best friend Cornelius's gold pocket watch. Me and Cornelius have been friends as long as I can remember; I would never take his watch. I've searched everywhere hoping to find it so we can be friends again, but I've had no such luck.

## Asking the PC to Keep Digging

`far18:` Would you really? Nobody will help me because they think I stole the watch. It would be nice to have somebody on my side, for once. I've already questioned everyone about it, but it won't hurt to ask everybody again. Maybe they'll tell you something they wouldn't tell me. It also won't hurt to poke yer nose around Rose's Bed and Breakfast; Cornelius is notorious for putting stuff down and forgetting where he put it a minute later.
