---

> **Direction:** Gruff, foul-mouthed bar owner with a short fuse — treats his customers like they're a nuisance he's tolerating, not a business he's grateful for. Quick to threaten violence over small disrespect ("shut your hole," "get out"), openly contemptuous of rivals (Becky's cheaper whiskey eats at him constantly), and casually crude when it comes to women. Underneath the bluster he's business-minded and transactional — he pays for information and results, and turns friendly the moment a deal works out for him.
