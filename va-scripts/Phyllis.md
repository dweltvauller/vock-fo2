# Phyllis — VA Script
**Character:** Phyllis, Resident Medical Assistant of Vault City

**Total recordable lines:** 66 (`phl1`–`phl66`)

---

> **Direction:** Adult female, early-to-mid twenties. Phyllis is Dr. Troy's medical assistant — bright, a little overworked, and fundamentally sheltered by a lifetime spent entirely inside Vault City's planned, artificial-insemination society. Play her default register as brisk and professional, warming into genuine friendliness with anyone she considers a proper Citizen. She's openly, comedically naive about anything the Vault's controlled culture doesn't cover — sex, reproduction, the outside world — and should sound earnestly confused rather than in on the joke. She has a temper if disrespected (sharp, offended, quick to call the guards) and a wistful, restless streak underneath the busywork — she's quietly tired of how uniform and dull life in the Vault is.
