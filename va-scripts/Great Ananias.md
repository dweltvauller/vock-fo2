# Great Ananias — VA Script
**Character:** The Great Ananias, huckster running a fake "mummy" exhibit, Den

**Total recordable lines:** 30 (`ana1`–`ana30`)

---

> **Direction:** The Great Ananias is a short, mustachioed carnival huckster who runs a scam "Egyptian mummy" exhibit out of a back room in the Den — really just a sleeping ghoul named Woody dressed up in a cheap sarcophagus prop. He's a classic showman: florid, theatrical language, a flourish and a bow for every greeting, always selling. Underneath the patter he's greedy and a little desperate. When the scam is running smoothly he's all confidence and charm; when he's challenged or the ghoul accusation comes up he gets defensive and blustery; once the scam is finally exposed and Woody bolts, he deflates into something genuinely pathetic — a con man watching his whole racket collapse. Vocal qualities: a huckster's sing-song patter, big and performative when he's "on," petulant and small when he's caught.

---

`ana2:` Hello, welcome, salutations! I am the Great Ananias (he offers you a slight bow, with a flourish). I bring mysteries from near and far friend. Mysteries from a world beyond our own... of the Netherworld.

*Repeat visits.*

`ana3:` Come back to learn of the Great Ananias' mysteries of the Netherworld have you?
