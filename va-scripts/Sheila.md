# Sheila — VA Script
**Character:** Sheila, a prostitute working the Hole, Den

**Total recordable lines:** 25 (`shela1`–`shela26`; 1 tag share another tag's recording)

---

> **Direction:** Streetwise and transactional — this is a business, and she treats it like one.
> Blunt, a little world-weary, quick with a sarcastic edge when someone wastes her time or can't
> pay. She warms up fast once money and mood are right — playful, teasing, even genuinely pleased
> with a good customer — then cools right back down to brisk and dismissive once the transaction's
> over. If a customer disappoints her, the sarcasm gets sharper rather than angrier. Play the arc as
> appraising → flirtatious/businesslike → satisfied (or openly unimpressed) → back to brisk.

---
