# Chris — VA Script
**Character:** Chris, Gas Station Attendant at Navarro

**Total recordable lines:** 39 (`chris1`–`chris39`)

---

> **Direction:** An old man, folksy and talkative on the surface -- the friendly small-town gas station attendant, quick with a "howdy, stranger" and happy to ramble about his service station. Underneath, he's an Enclave lookout guarding the hidden base, and he gets cagey and evasive the moment Navarro or vertibirds come up. Press him and the folksy act cracks fast: bluffing turns to threats, and if he's caught out or cornered he goes straight to calling for armed backup over the radio, then fighting. Play the arc as good-natured old-timer → cagey and defensive → openly hostile.

---
