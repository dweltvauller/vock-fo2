# T-Ray — VA Script
**Character:** T-Ray, chop-shop hustler, New Reno

**Total recordable lines:** 104 (`tray1`–`tray104`)

---

> **Direction:** Adult Black male, streetwise New Reno hustler running a chop shop out of the back lots. Cocky and constantly flirtatious/sexually boastful — he talks about his car and his sex life in the same breath, and means it as a brag either way. Quick, confident patter as a negotiator: he never gives ground without getting something back (cash, batteries, "favors"), and he clearly enjoys the back-and-forth. That confidence turns hard and threatening fast the moment a deal falls through or he's disrespected — "chop shop boy" territory, genuine violence just under the surface. Heavy AAVE-inflected slang throughout ("aiight," "bitness," "shore as hell," "y'know?") — play it naturally, not exaggerated.
