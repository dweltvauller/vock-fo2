# Amanda — VA Script
**Character:** Amanda, Vault City Courtyard

**Total recordable lines:** 21 (`ama1`–`ama21`)

---

> **Direction:** Amanda is a young wife and mother camped in the Vault City courtyard, worn down by fear and poverty. Her husband Joshua was arrested after a bar fight and is being held as a Servant unless she can pay the guards or find another way to free him. Play her as anxious and pleading throughout — a woman trying to hold herself together in front of a stranger. She's guarded and suspicious at first (especially toward anyone who seems hostile or unsympathetic), opens up once she senses the PC might actually help, and grows increasingly desperate as the conversation turns to what she can offer in exchange for that help — flustered and ashamed, not flirtatious. If she ultimately agrees to a private arrangement to secure the money, that shame carries into her later lines, where she's anxious the PC might mention it in front of Joshua. Once Joshua is actually freed, let real relief and gratitude come through for the first time.
