# Lo Pan — VA Script
**Character:** Lo Pan, Shi gang boss, arch-enemy of the Dragon (San Francisco)

**Total recordable lines:** 45 (`lopan1`–`lopan45`)

---

> **Direction:** Adult male, arrogant and domineering, a martial-arts master who thinks in terms of strength and weakness. Cold and contemptuous by default, especially toward anyone he reads as weak, preachy, or "self-righteous." Warms up (slightly) toward anyone who proves themselves tough, and turns openly respectful once they've actually beaten him or his men. Underneath the swagger is a real, bitter rivalry with the Dragon — talk of him should carry genuine old-grudge heat, not cartoon villainy.

---
