# Valerie — VA Script
**Character:** Valerie, Vic's daughter, who runs the workshop in Vault City's courtyard

**Total recordable lines:** 24 (`vcval47`–`vcval70`)

> **Casting note:** Valerie's dialogue (`VCVAL1`–`VCVAL46`) was recorded for the Talking Heads (THAT) mod. These 24 lines are the floats THAT never voiced. Ideally they're recorded by the same actor so Valerie has one voice throughout; if that isn't possible, match the existing THAT recordings as closely as you can.

---

> **Direction:** Adult woman, a sharp, capable mechanic with a dry, sarcastic edge. Tough and self-reliant, quick with a put-down, but not cruel. With her father Vic she swings between real anger at him for walking out on the family and the grudging affection underneath. These are floats spoken over the action, so keep them punchy.
