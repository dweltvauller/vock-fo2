# Darion — VA Script
**Character:** Darion, leader of the New Khans (Vault 15)

**Total recordable lines:** 3 (`dario1`-`dario3`) — one taunt, three variants by the player
character's karma when they reach him. Only one plays per playthrough. `dario2` and `dario3` are
VOCK additions written by C. Jared Castor.

---

> **Direction:** Darion is the boss the player has been building up to — old, gray-haired, but clearly still dangerous and completely unimpressed. He's sarcastic and contemptuous from the first word, no matter who is standing in front of him. No fear, no negotiation — he's already decided how this ends. Play each line as one escalating breath: dry, theatrical mockery curdling into open hostility, landing hard and flat on the order to attack.

---

## First Confrontation — Good Karma (vanilla)
*Player finally reaches Darion. He greets a well-regarded PC with mocking praise before turning cold and giving the kill order.*

`dario1:` So, here you are at last. The savior of the downtrodden, the righter of wrongs, the icon of hope for humanity. What a dumb-ass! Well, before you start any boring speeches, I'd just like to say that I'm not fucking interested. Let's just cut to the chase and get this over with. Baddog, kill!

## First Confrontation — Bad Karma
*Same moment, but the PC has a track record of bad karma. Darion isn't impressed — he sees a pup playing at being a monster.*

`dario2:` Well, if it isn't the new scourge of the wastes. You crawl out from behind some rock, leave a few blood puddles here and there, and you think you're the devil's own bastard son? You're not badass, you're nothing but a puppy. How's about you meet mine? Baddog, kill!

## First Confrontation — Neutral Karma
*Same moment, but the PC hasn't built a reputation either way. Darion doesn't know or care who this is.*

`dario3:` Who the fuck are you? No... better question — who the fuck do you think you are? You barge into my house, you gun down my people, and somehow you expect me to give a shit about whatever you've come here to say? You're nothing. A nobody. So let's not waste each other's time pretending this is some grand showdown. I'm bored, you're out of your depth, and this is where your story ends. Baddog... kill.

*End of script — 3 lines total, one plays per playthrough. Tag numbers follow ascending message-ID order.*
