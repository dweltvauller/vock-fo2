# Cornelius — VA Script
**Character:** Cornelius, elderly resident of Modoc, nominal proprietor of Rose's Bed and Breakfast

**Total recordable lines:** 38 (`corn1`–`corn38`)

---

> **Direction:** An elderly, genially senile old man who thinks he runs "Rose's Bed and Breakfast" — his wife Rose actually does the real work, a fact he keeps forgetting and re-explaining. Voice should read warm, doddering, and a little scattered: he loses his train of thought mid-sentence, contradicts himself, and rambles off into unrelated town gossip. That warmth curdles fast the moment his stolen gold watch or his best friend Farrel comes up — he turns defensive and a little paranoid on the subject, and once he's convinced Farrel really did steal from him, the doddering old man gives way to real fury, wanting Farrel strung up. If told Farrel was murdered, he can tip into open hostility and start calling for a lynching himself. Play the arc as confused and sweet → paranoid and hurt → openly vengeful.

---
