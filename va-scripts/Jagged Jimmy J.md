# Jagged Jimmy J — VA Script
**Character:** Jagged Jimmy J, street-corner Jet dealer, New Reno (ncjimmyj)

**Total recordable lines:** 63 (`jimmy1`–`jimmy63`)

---

> **Direction:** Adult male, a streetwise New Reno drug dealer with fast, slangy hustler patter -- always working an angle, addressing the player as "my pilot friend." Genuinely proud of his product and of his reputation for keeping customers happy (and alive). Warm and eager to please on the surface, quick to launch into a sales pitch. The one thing that cracks the routine is any suggestion he had a hand in poisoning the Wright boy -- there he turns defensive fast, then openly hostile and threatening, before circling back to wounded self-justification.
