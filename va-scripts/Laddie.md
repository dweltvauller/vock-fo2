# Laddie — VA Script
**Character:** Laddie, Jonny's Dog (Modoc)

**Total recordable lines:** 6 (`lad1`–`lad6`)

---

> **Direction:** A border collie — alert, intelligent, clearly smarter than the average dog (the game says so outright). No words, just expressive dog vocalizations: an eager, friendly triple-bark as his default greeting, an aggressive growl-and-lunge if he doesn't like you, and a short, quizzical "Woof?" that should read as almost mocking — like he's laughing at you. Keep it a real dog's vocal range, not cartoonish.

---

## Greeting Bark
*Laddie's default bark when the PC first approaches him, whether he's just met them, is already following them, or is waiting at the well. The game describes him as "highly agitated": excited and worked up, not friendly yet (the happy version is `lad6` below).*

`lad3:` Woof! Woof! Woof!

## Petting Response — He Likes You
*PC pets him and he responds warmly. Laddie jumps up and licks the PC's face while barking: happy, playful barks, a different take from the agitated greeting (`lad3`).*

`lad6:` Woof! Woof! Woof! [Laddie jumps on you and starts to lick your face.]

## Petting Response — He Doesn't Like You
*PC pets him but has a bad reputation with him — he turns aggressive instead.*

`lad4:` Grrrr! [Laddie takes a vicious bite at you.]

## Yes Bark
*Affirmative bark, used throughout the "guess what happened to Jonny" back-and-forth whenever the answer is yes.*

`lad2:` Woof! Woof!

## No / Acknowledge Bark
*Single short bark — used both as a plain "no" during the guessing game and as a general acknowledgment (e.g. after the PC agrees to go rescue Jonny).*

`lad1:` Woof!

## Questioning / Mocking Bark
*A single quizzical "Woof?" — used once when the PC's been needling him ("Nobody likes a smart ass") and again later in the guessing game once the PC's run out of guesses. Same bark both times; the first instance carries a bit more of a mocking edge.*

`lad5:` Woof? [You can almost hear the dog laughing at you.]

`lad5:` Woof?

---

*End of script*
