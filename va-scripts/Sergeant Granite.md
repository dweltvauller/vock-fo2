# Sergeant Granite — VA Script
**Character:** Sergeant Granite, EC Company checkpoint guard (Enclave oil rig)

**Total recordable lines:** 34 (`grani1`–`grani34`)

---

> **Direction:** Adult male, a hard-nosed Enclave grunt standing checkpoint duty. Suspicious and gruff with a stranger at first -- a soldier sizing up a threat, quick with tough-guy banter and dark gallows humor about the monster he's watching the door for (Frank Horrigan). Play the warnings about Horrigan as a mix of genuine unease and macho bravado -- he's rattled by what Horrigan does to people, but won't admit it outright. Turns openly skeptical and mocking when the player claims the reactor's melting down, daring them to keep talking. If the player wins him over, the tough-guy front cracks into something more sincere -- a soldier throwing in with a stranger because it's the right call, not because he's been tricked into it. The dismissive lines to a slow-witted player are a weary drill sergeant brushing off someone he's already written off; the post-recruitment barks are a squad leader rallying his men for a fight.
