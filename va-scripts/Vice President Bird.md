# Vice President Bird — VA Script
**Character:** Vice-President Daniel Bird, Enclave Oil Rig

**Total recordable lines:** 45 (`bird1`–`bird45`)

---

> **Direction:** A small, nervous-looking man in a food-stained suit. On the surface he's twitchy and easily flustered, but the moment the conversation turns to policy he switches into a droning, over-rehearsed political cadence, delivering total non-sequiturs and malapropisms with complete sincerity, like a man who's said these lines so many times he no longer hears how strange they sound. If actually threatened, that confidence cracks fast: a burst of hollow bravado, then a full mental collapse into disjointed, childlike, traumatized muttering. Play the arc as jittery functionary → droning politician → rattled bravado → total breakdown.
