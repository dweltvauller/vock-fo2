# Chieftain — VA Script
**Character:** The elderly chief of the Umbra Tribe (Primitive Tribe village)

**Total recordable lines:** 15 (`chief1`–`chief15`)

---

> **Direction:** An ancient tribal elder — small, weathered, pipe-smoking, speaking in slow, deliberate cadences full of talk of "the spirits" and "bonespirits." He's warm toward the PC, calling them "child," but there's real gravity underneath: he's a leader who's lost people and expects to lose more. Grave and unhurried when the topic turns to Sulik's fate, especially the line where he admits he once hoped Sulik might succeed him. Curious and a little wistful discussing the "Holy GECK" and things beyond his understanding. Near the end of the game he turns almost ceremonial and portentous promising a "final great secret" — building real weight and suspense before the bit lands as a deadpan gag (his pipe sets him on fire mid-sentence). The lone float is a sudden, sharp change of tone: a fierce shout at a PC he recognizes as a slaver, right before he attacks. Male voice, elderly, unhurried pace, no particular real-world accent — just an old man's gravel.

## Greeting the PC

*First visit, and Kurisu is already safely home with the tribe.*

`chief1:` [The old man smokes his pipe and watches you with dull gray eyes that seem to bore into your soul. He motions for you to sit across from him in the smoke-filled tent.] Thank you for bringing Kurisu back to our village. [He takes a long hit off his pipe.] The spirits... they have much to say about you. Much indeed. Tell me, is there something you need?

*First visit, otherwise.*

`chief2:` [The old man smokes his pipe and watches you with dull gray eyes that seem to bore into your soul. He motions for you to sit across from him in the smoke-filled tent.] We are most grateful for your services, Wanderer. [He takes a long hit off his pipe.] The spirits... they have much to say about you. Much indeed. Tell me, what brings you to our village?

*A later, return visit.*

`chief3:` Welcome back, child. Was there something else you needed?

*Asking a further question within the same conversation.*

`chief4:` What can this old man do for you?

## The Holy GECK

`chief5:` The Holy GECK... [He takes a long hit off his pipe.] There is a creature that we sometimes hunt -- sometimes, it hunts us. A gecko. No, I sense that's not what you seek. I'm afraid your Holy GECK is beyond my ken.

## Asking About Sulik

*Opening the topic of Sulik.*

`chief6:` Sulik? [He nods.] One of our best men. Equally skilled at the art of the hunt, and the art of communing with bonespirits. He left us to find his sister, Kurisu, who never returned from a nearby tribe. Tell me, child, where is Sulik now?

*Sulik is right there with the PC.*

`chief7:` [Sulik and the tribe's chief exchange greetings.] It brings me great pleasure to see Sulik again. The bonespirits told me he had fallen into trouble. But what of his sister, Kurisu?

*Sulik isn't present, but is alive and well.*

`chief8:` It is good to hear that Sulik is well. We were worried he got into trouble. So, his sister is still missing?
 She too is a member of our tribe.

*The PC has told him Sulik is dead.*

`chief9:` [He frowns and smokes his pipe in silence for a long time. Just when you consider leaving. He finally speaks.] The spirits were strong with Sulik. I always thought, he might one day take my place. Now, I see the spirits had other plans for Sulik. But what of his sister, Kurisu?

*Telling him Kurisu was taken by slavers.*

`chief10:` [He closes his eyes for a long time. Just as you think he might have fallen asleep, he begins speaking.] The slavers are led by a man with a dark soul. Metzger. He dwells in one of the ruined husks of the Old One's cities. They call it the Den. [He opens his eyes.] Perhaps, he is holding her there. The bonespirits say no more.

## Nothing Else Needed

`chief11:` Thank you, child, but I am content. Perhaps others are not so. Ask around the village.

## The Final Great Secret
*After the PC has defeated the Enclave, on a later visit.*

`chief12:` [The old man smokes his pipe and watches you with dull grey eyes that seem to bore into your soul. He motions for you to sit across from him in the smoke-filled tent.] You have done a great deed, child. I hear the cries of many wicked bonespirits lost out in the Great Salt Water. Now that you have proven yourself, I can share with you the final great secret.

`chief13:` [He takes a big hit off his pipe, then closes his eyes. After a moment, he nods.] Yes. I see the Great Bear spirit has guided your quest, breathing new life into old bones. Others as well. Perhaps they've come to you in a vision. Come from far, far away... where mutants are not allowed.

`chief14:` The final secret... the greatest secret in the entire game... is, [As he does a dramatic pause, he takes another hit off his pipe. Suddenly, the pipe slips from his hands and falls into his robes, instantly igniting him in flames.]
