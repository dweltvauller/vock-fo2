# Chip — VA Script
**Character:** Chip, punk with a missing spleen, San Francisco (the tanker crew)

**Total recordable lines:** 32 (`chip1`–`chip32`)

---

> **Direction:** A young adult San Francisco punk in constant physical pain, having drunkenly gambled away his own spleen in a bet he can't remember agreeing to. Whiny, self-pitying, and quick to curse at anyone who mocks him or turns him down. He warms considerably once the PC agrees to track the spleen down and arrange to get it put back — grateful, a little pathetic about it, genuinely scared of dying without it. Once it's reinserted he's a completely different guy: no longer in pain, breezy and businesslike, happy to talk tanker-crew logistics (the Captain, computer work, his missing people). If the PC crosses the San Francisco punks, he turns openly hostile. Play the arc as pained and bitter → grateful and needy → (once healed) easygoing and helpful → (if betrayed) hostile.

---
