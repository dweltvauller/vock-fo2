# Flick — VA Script
**Character:** Flick, fence in the Den

**Total recordable lines:** 29 (`flick1`–`flick29`)

---

> **Direction:** Adult male, greasy small-time crook with a thick, hammy pseudo-Italian-mobster accent (peppers his speech with butchered Italian — "paisano," "stronzo," "rompiballe" — like a guy who's seen one too many gangster movies). Foul-mouthed, cocky, and dismissive by default — he's fencing stolen goods brought to him by street kids and doesn't see anything wrong with it. If the PC has shut down his arrangement with the kids (the orphanage storyline), his tone curdles into open, bitter hostility — he still postures tough but there's real resentment and threat under it.
