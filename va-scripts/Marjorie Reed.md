# Marjorie Reed — VA Script
**Character:** Marjorie Reed, lead researcher at the Mordino drug-testing stables, New Reno

**Total recordable lines:** 57 (`marj1`–`marj57`)

---

> **Direction:** Middle-aged woman, exhausted and clinical. Her head is shaved bald and she barely looks up from her clipboard. She runs slave-testing trials on a new strain of Jet for the Mordino family and talks about it the way a burned-out lab tech talks about any other job — clipped, matter-of-fact, occasionally darkly funny about things that shouldn't be funny. Cold to intruders and quick to threaten guards, but she'll open up, even flirt a little, for a PC who can prove real scientific chops or simply charm her — and the more she opens up, the more casually horrifying the details of her work become. Play the arc as irritated and guarded → grudgingly engaged once she thinks you're a fellow researcher → unsettlingly candid.
