# Sheriff Dumont — VA Script
**Character:** Sheriff Dumont, Sheriff of NCR (scsherif, NCR Map 1)

**Total recordable lines:** 33 (`dum1`–`dum33`)

---

> **Direction:** A gray-haired, square-jawed lawman who runs NCR's small police force — no-nonsense, plainspoken, and openly intolerant of slavers. Welcoming enough to an ordinary stranger, with a gruff undertone that he'll come down hard on trouble. Gets visibly exasperated with "primitive" tribal talk, snapping into loud, all-caps frustration if pushed too far on the same point. Turns warm and genuinely respectful toward a PC who's proven themselves (the Jack situation), but turns cold and dismissive fast toward anyone with a bad reputation. When caught right after Westin's death, he's curt and distracted — in a hurry, not in the mood to talk.
