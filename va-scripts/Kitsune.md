# Kitsune — VA Script
**Character:** Kitsune, EPA specimen / companion (epac12)

**Total recordable lines:** 202 (`kit1`–`kit202`)

---

> **Direction:** Adult woman, late 20s/early 30s in appearance, though she was frozen for over a century. Found disoriented and vulnerable in an EPA cryo tank -- start her scared, stammering, physically weak (chattering teeth, catching her breath) and play that off gradually as she gets her bearings. Underneath, she's a trained spy: guarded, deflecting, careful about what she reveals, with a dry, wry sense of humor that surfaces once she's steadier on her feet. Her backstory is heavy -- she was complicit in triggering the Great War -- so the storytelling sections should carry real weight and a little guilt, without ever losing her composure entirely; she's someone who has made peace with a terrible thing and is still working out what that means. Once recruited, she settles into a competent, dryly affectionate party member: capable, a little sardonic, protective of the people she travels with. Play the arc as frightened and fragile -> guarded and testing -> confessional -> settled, capable travel companion.

---
