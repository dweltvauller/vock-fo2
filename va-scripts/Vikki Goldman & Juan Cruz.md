# Vikki Goldman & Juan Cruz — VA Script
**Characters:** Vikki Goldman and Juan Cruz, ex-porn-star Hubologist recruiters at the San Francisco Elron camp

**Total recordable lines:** 49 (`vaj1`–`vaj49`)

---

> **Direction:** Two voices, one scene — read as a practiced double act. Juan ("Him") and Vikki ("Her") finish each other's sentences and trade off mid-thought; most lines are marked `Him:`/`Her:`/`Both:` and should be read as a genuine back-and-forth between two actors, not a single narrator switching hats. Default tone is chipper, over-rehearsed infomercial cheer — glossy, a little too eager, leaning on their minor-celebrity status ("we're celebrities!") like it's their whole personality. Underneath the sales pitch is a controlling, faintly menacing edge: cross them (insult the Hub, refuse to join, get caught snooping) and the cheer snaps into cold threats without much of a transition. Once someone's fully recruited, the tone shifts again into smug, proprietary warmth — and, with Dave, a jealous, oddly horny undercurrent that Juan tries (and fails) to play off as "negative energy." Play the arc as glossy pitch-people → sharp threat → self-satisfied cult handlers.
