# Tubby — VA Script
**Character:** Tubby, shop owner, the Den

**Total recordable lines:** 22 (`tubby1`–`tubby22`)

---

> **Direction:** Tubby is the sleazy, paranoid, and opportunistic shop owner in the Den. He operates a small storefront where he trades goods and peddles Jet. He is sharp, purely transactional, and treats people based on how much coin they carry. He frequently deals with local junkies like Petey, treating them with a mix of impatience and cold realism.
