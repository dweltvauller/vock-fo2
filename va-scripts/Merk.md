# Merk — VA Script
**Character:** Merk, "The Man" — crime boss of NCR's Rawhide

**Total recordable lines:** 87 (`merk1`–`merk87`)

---

> **Direction:** Heavy-set, well-dressed adult male with an air of arrogant, deliberate coldness — a man who moves slowly because he's never in a hurry for anyone. He runs the Rawhide and treats every conversation as a transaction: information costs money, jobs are handed out only once you've proven useful, and threats are delivered in the same flat, unbothered tone as small talk. He's crude and a little lecherous toward women, openly contemptuous of anyone he judges weak or stupid, and backed by muscle (his enforcer Lenny and "the boys") he's happy to sic on you. Under the cool surface is real menace — when he decides to stop being civil, he goes cold and businesslike about violence rather than loud about it. Play the arc as bored crime boss → transactional → openly threatening once you cross him.
