# Louis Salvatore — VA Script
**Character:** Louis Salvatore, head of New Reno's Salvatore family, an old man who breathes through a respirator

**Total recordable lines:** 14 (`salva40`–`salva53`)

> **Casting note:** Louis Salvatore's dialogue (`SALVA1`–`SALVA39`) was recorded for the Talking Heads (THAT) mod. These lines are floats THAT never voiced. Ideally they're recorded by the same actor so the character has one voice throughout; if that isn't possible, match the existing THAT recordings as closely as you can.

---

> **Direction:** Old, frail and deadly. A cold, formal crime boss whose lungs are failing; every line fights through a wheeze or a cough. Quiet menace rather than volume.

---
