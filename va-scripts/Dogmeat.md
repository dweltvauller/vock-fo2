# Dogmeat — VA Script
**Character:** Dogmeat, the stray mutt at the Klamath junkyard (later a party member)

**Total recordable lines:** 11 (`dogmt1`–`dogmt11`)

---

> **Direction:** Dogmeat is a real dog, not a talking companion — every line here is a vocalization to perform, not words to read aloud. Most of the script text describes the sound in the third person ("the dog gives a low whine," "a sharp bark, then a low growl") rather than spelling it out phonetically; treat that description as the performance note for what to vocalize, not as something to voice literally. He's a "mean-looking" stray who's actually eager and loyal underneath — friendly and hopeful once he's decided to trust the PC, obedient and alert as a party member taking commands, wary and growly toward anyone he doesn't trust yet, and genuinely distressed (a real whimper, not a bark) when hurt or sick.

---

## Wants to Join
*The PC is wearing a vault suit — Dogmeat recognizes it and wants to tag along. Perform the whine described, not the sentence itself.*

`dogmt1:` The dog gives a low whine when it sees the vault suit. It looks like it wants to come with you.

## Given a Treat
*The PC feeds Dogmeat before he's decided to join — an iguana-on-a-stick or a can of dog food. Perform the eating sound, satisfied and greedy.*

`dogmt2:` The dog greedily swallows the iguana on a stick, then looks at you expectantly.

`dogmt6:` The dog greedily swallows the contents of the can of dog food, then looks at you expectantly.

## Party Hub Greeting
*Dogmeat's standard acknowledgment when talked to as an active party member, and again when told to stop waiting and rejoin — same take both times.*

`dogmt3:` You see one meeeean-looking dog.

## Told to Wait Here
*Dogmeat is left behind to wait. A long, resigned howl.*

`dogmt5:` Awooooooooooo

## Follow Distance Confirmed
*The PC sets how closely Dogmeat should follow. Read as "got it," not aggression.*

`dogmt4:` Dogmeat gives a sharp bark, then a low growl.

`dogmt10:` Awooooooooooo! *wimper*
