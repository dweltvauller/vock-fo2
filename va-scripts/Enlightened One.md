# Enlightened One — VA Script
**Character:** The Enlightened One, a Hubologist preacher in NCR

**Total recordable lines:** 47 (`eo1`–`eo47`)

---

> **Direction:** Adult woman, a member of the Church of Hubology stationed on her own in NCR. Speaks with a serene, spacey cult-preacher calm -- gentle, blissed-out, faintly detached from reality, treating every stranger as a potential convert ("child," "seeker," "fellow seeker"). That calm cracks fast: mock her beliefs and she goes from tranquil to sharply wounded and indignant in a single line. Confronted about the contract on her life, she doesn't panic -- she stays eerily composed, almost resigned, still trying to talk her way out of it as a matter of principle rather than fear. Play the arc as beatific calm → wounded/offended → resigned but firm.

---
