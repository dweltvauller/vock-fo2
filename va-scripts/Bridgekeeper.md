# Bridgekeeper — VA Script
**Character:** The Keeper of the Bridge of Death — special random encounter (Monty Python and the Holy Grail parody)

**Total recordable lines:** 17 (`brige1`–`brige17`)

---

> **Direction:** A straight lift of the Bridgekeeper from Monty Python and the Holy Grail. He is wild-eyed, with an unkempt beard and a raspy, cracked old voice. His left eye looks ready to jump out of his head. At the bridge he is a menacing gatekeeper: theatrical, sing-song, and enjoying the power of life and death over every traveler. The questions are an incantation, not small talk. Hold that menace through the trivia questions, which are deliberately absurd Fallout trivia read with the same doom-laden gravity as "What is your name?". When the PC stumps him, the menace collapses into a panicked stammer. After the PC passes, he drops the act entirely and shows the tired, put-upon actor underneath. He mutters about his agent and his "cheese gigs" as he wanders off. Keep the rasp throughout, including the off-duty grumbling.

---

## The Challenge
*The PC approaches the bridge.*

`brige1:` Stop! Who would cross the Bridge of Death must answer me these questions three, 'ere the other side he see.

## Question One

`brige2:` What is your name?

## Question Two

`brige3:` What is your quest?

## Question Three
*One of three questions, picked at random.*

**Fallout 1 trivia.** *Two versions of one question. The longer version plays for a low-Intelligence PC.*

`brige4:` Which non-player character from Fallout 1 survived to appear in the sequel, although the said person may be now very old?

`brige5:` Which non-player character from Fallout 1 survived to appear in the sequel?

**Carry weight.**

`brige6:` How much weight can a person with a strength of six carry assuming he has the Strong Back perk?

**Perk requirement.**

`brige7:` What is the minimum requirement for the Quick Pockets perk?

## The PC Answers Correctly

`brige8:` Right. Off you go.

## The PC Stumps Him
*The PC turns a question back on him ("NPC allies or NPC enemies?"), or a low-Intelligence PC babbles something he cannot parse. He has no answer, and the bridge takes him instead. His scream (`brige17`) follows.*

`brige9:` Uh... I... I don't know that!

## The PC Answers Wrong
*Floats over his head as the PC is disintegrated. Quiet, satisfied.*

`brige10:` Heh-heh.

## Walking Off
*Floats as he walks away after the PC passes. `brige11` plays first, once. `brige12`–`brige16` then cycle in order, about every 3 seconds, until he leaves the map. Off-duty: tired, sulky, muttering to himself.*

`brige11:` I never get to ask the good questions.

`brige12:` I am going to talk to my agent about all this.

`brige13:` I keep telling him I have to stop doing these cheese gigs.

`brige14:` How am I ever going to get respect that I deserve being in low budget movies and video games.

`brige15:` Hmmm... I should really think about getting into car repair.

`brige16:` I hear that is where all the money is made these days.

## Thrown Off the Bridge
*Floats over his head as he is hurled into the gorge after `brige9`. A long scream that fades into the distance.*

`brige17:` AAAAAAAHHHHHHHHhhhhhh!
