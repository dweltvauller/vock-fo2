# Matthew — VA Script
**Character:** Matthew, the Brotherhood of Steel's contact in San Francisco

**Total recordable lines:** 4 (`mat32`–`mat35`)

> **Casting note:** Matthew's dialogue (`MAT1`–`MAT31`) was recorded for the Talking Heads (THAT) mod. These 4 lines are from a cutscene THAT never voiced. Ideally they're recorded by the same actor so Matthew has one voice throughout; if that isn't possible, match the existing THAT recordings as closely as you can.

---

> **Direction:** Adult male, a calm, principled Brotherhood operative. Here he's facing death and knows it: defiant and steady, never begging.

---

## Death at Horrigan's Hands (San Francisco)

*A cutscene (the ACE computer vignette). Two days after the player finishes the Brotherhood quest, Frank Horrigan and two Enclave guards corner Matthew. He stands up to Horrigan, grabs a plasma rifle and shoots him twice, uselessly; Horrigan laughs and kills him. Horrigan's lines are shown as cues.*

`mat32:` You and your so-called President will not succeed.

*Horrigan: "Is that so?"*

`mat33:` The Chosen One has come, and he will rid the world of your evil.

*Horrigan: "I care nothing for your pathetic prophecies. Worse, you have become a nuisance through your meddling. That ends today!"*

`mat34:` Killing me will stop nothing.

*Horrigan: "That may be, but seeing your body rotting on the ground will bring a smile to my face."*

*Matthew grabs a plasma rifle.*

*Horrigan: "What do you think you're doing now?"*

*He shoots Horrigan twice. It barely registers.*

*Horrigan: "HA HA HA! You pathetic fool! HA HA HA!"*

*Horrigan: "Wow, I haven't laughed like that for a while. Anyway, get ready to die."*

*Matthew, terrified, a tiny whimper of a sound:*

`mat35:` ...peep...
