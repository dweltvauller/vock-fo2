# Myron -- VA Script
**Character:** Myron, the teenage Jet inventor from the Stables, New Reno (recruitable companion)

**Total recordable lines:** 6 (`myron1`-`myron6`)

> **Casting note:** Myron's dialogue was recorded for the original Fallout 2 (vanilla `MYN*` files). These 6 lines are floats the original game never voiced. Ideally they're recorded by the original actor so Myron has one voice throughout; if that isn't possible, match the original recordings as closely as you can.

---

> **Direction:** Myron is a cocky, mouthy teenage chemist who invented Jet and never lets anyone forget how brilliant he is. Arrogant, whiny and sarcastic, with a young man's bravado that he can't quite back up. Even a single word should carry his smug, nasal attitude.
