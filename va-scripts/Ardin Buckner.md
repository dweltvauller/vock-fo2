# Ardin Buckner — VA Script
**Character:** Ardin Buckner, owner of Buckner House (the inn), Klamath

**Total recordable lines:** 55 (`ardin1`–`ardin56`; 1 tag share another tag's recording)

---

> **Direction:** Ardin is a sturdy, warm-hearted innkeeper in her fifties — practical, a little
> weary, but fundamentally kind. She runs Buckner House alone while worrying about two people she
> loves: her son Torr (sweet-natured but simple) and Smiley, a trapper she's been courting who's
> gone missing on "one last quest." Her tone shifts with the situation — welcoming and businesslike
> at the front desk, quietly anxious as the missing-persons plot develops, openly grief-stricken if
> either is reported dead, and fiercely protective (bordering on vengeful) once she learns the
> Dunton brothers have been terrorizing Torr. She has a blunt, unwelcoming edge for tribals and
> troublemakers, and a rare cold fury if the PC is the one who hurt Torr.

---
