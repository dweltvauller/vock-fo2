# Rebecca (Vault 15) — VA Script
**Character:** Rebecca, a squatter mother at the Vault 15 entrance

**Total recordable lines:** 26 (`rb151`–`rb1526`)

---

> **Direction:** Rebecca is an older woman who has lived a hard life but hasn't lost her warmth. She's a mother first — her daughter Chrissy has gone missing, and that fear colors everything she says. She opens guarded and paranoid, whispering, convinced she's being watched, and won't talk business out in the open. Once she trusts the player and gets somewhere private, she becomes open, grateful, and a little desperate — a woman asking a stranger for help because she has nowhere else to turn. Later in the conversation she's businesslike answering questions about the Squat, but always ready to snap back to anxious mother the moment Chrissy comes up. If Chrissy is found, she's overwhelmed with relief and gratitude, curdling into quiet anger and betrayal once she learns the truth about Darion.

---

*Guarded and whispering — she doesn't want to be overheard.*

`rb151:` Hi, I'm Rebecca. Can we talk privately, please?

`rb152:` (whispering) My daughter is missing and I need help finding her. I can't talk about it here, though. Can we go somewhere private?

`rb153:` (whispering) You may think I'm crazy but I get the feeling I'm being watched all the time. It's rather disturbing, and I'm a bit on edge because of it.

## Reaching the Tent
*Relief at finally having somewhere safe to talk.*

`rb154:` Thanks; we can go to my tent.

`rb155:` We should be able to talk here.

## The Missing Daughter
*The full plea — a worried mother laying it all out.*

`rb156:` I have a daughter named Chrissy who's a bit of a tomboy. She likes to run around, explore, and pretty much stick her nose into things. Chrissy's a good kid, though. She doesn't cause trouble, she's always home on time and she's shown herself to be a very responsible young lady. I let her do as she pleases most of the time. Last week Chrissy didn't come home. I've asked around, and nobody's seen or heard from her. I tried to find her myself, but Zeke, he's... sort of the mayor here... stopped me. He said it wasn't safe for me to go out looking on my own; he'd have some of his men do it. They couldn't find her, either, and said that maybe the slavers got her.

## Asking Again
*If the player comes back before deciding whether to help.*

`rb1522:` I can't explain why, but I don't believe the slavers took her. I've an uneasy feeling that something else is going on. And then there's this feeling that I'm being followed. I've had it ever since I talked to Zeke about Chrissy. I need someone to help me find my daughter. Will you help me, please?

## Ready to Help
*Businesslike once the player agrees.*

`rb157:` What do you want to know?

## About the Squat and the NCR
`rb158:` We call this place the 'Squat' because it was settled by the homeless of many cities. It's not much, but it's all we have. To the west is a city called the NCR. They've known about us for quite some time, and never offered us any kind of help or hindrance. Now, they are suddenly claiming this area as their ancestral home, and they want it back.

*If asked again on a later pass.*

`rb1523:` They keep sending people here to try and talk us out of our home, but they don't understand that we have nowhere else to go. We don't have the skills necessary to survive on our own if we leave here.

## About the Vault
`rb159:` Well... there is a vault here; I think it's number 15. They want to loot it for any of the old technology that may still be there.

## Who Runs Things
*Pointing the player toward Zeke.*

`rb1510:` That would be Zeke. He runs everything up here. You can find him in the building over there. ::points north-east::

## About Vault 13
`rb1511:` I'm sorry, I have no idea. I would ask about it in NCR. They've taken a great interest in vaults lately.

## About the Vault Repairs
`rb1512:` Well... we're not supposed to talk about it, but since I need your help... The vault is being repaired by Darion. He's our benefactor. When it's fully functional, we're all supposed to move in where we'll be safe from raiders, slavers, and such. So far he has the food and water systems up, but that's about it.

*If asked again on a later pass.*

Anyway, Darion doesn't want anyone to know what's going on with the vault. He's afraid that someone will take it all away from us if they find out. So, we're told to drive off anyone who shows up here -- especially the NCR.

## About Darion
`rb1513:` There's not much to say, really. Darion keeps to himself and never leaves the vault. If he has anything to say, he says it through Zeke. I've only met him once, and he seemed like a nice guy.
