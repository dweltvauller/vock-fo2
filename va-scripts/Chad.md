# Chad — VA Script
**Character:** Chad, Caravan Master of Broken Hills

**Total recordable lines:** 36 (`chad1`–`chad36`)

---

> **Direction:** Adult male, friendly traveling-salesman charm on the surface — glib, quick with a smile, good with people. He's quietly embezzling caravan money meant for Broken Hills, so that charm should read as a little too smooth, like he's always closing a deal. Once he's cornered (caught with the stolen money, or accused outright), the friendliness drops fast: he turns cold, then openly hostile and threatening, then panicked and pleading once he realizes he's beaten. Play the arc as salesman → cornered rat → desperate.
